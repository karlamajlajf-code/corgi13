# Improved Prompt: Tejo Beauty — Attention-Grabbing Positioning + 90‑Day Competitive Win Plan

## Objective

Create a high-impact go-to-market and messaging upgrade for **Tejo Beauty** (beauty products importer; **retail + wholesale** ecommerce; based in Croatia) that grabs attention quickly and converts.

## Scope

### A) Competitor Deconstruction ("how they win")

Analyze direct and adjacent competitors that a customer would realistically compare Tejo Beauty against.

- **Direct EU beauty ecommerce retailers** (examples to include): Notino, Douglas, Sephora (EU markets), Lookfantastic / The Hut Group.
- **Adjacent/structural competitors**: Amazon marketplaces, regional drugstore chains with ecommerce (varies by country), price-comparison behavior.
- **Wholesale/B2B alternatives**: B2B marketplaces (Faire, Ankorstore) and any relevant beauty wholesale/distributor patterns.

For each competitor, produce:

- **Monetization model**: margin structure, private label, loyalty/subscriptions, bundles, shipping thresholds, promos, B2B pricing, upsells.
- **Growth model**: SEO topics, paid channels, influencer/affiliate loops, email/SMS, loyalty, referral, review flywheel.
- **Funnel**: landing -> product discovery -> trust -> checkout -> retention.
- **Weaknesses**: what they do poorly (localization gaps, trust, wholesale friction, support, stock transparency, compliance clarity, shipping/returns pain).

### B) Win Strategy ("how we beat them")

Convert competitor weaknesses into **Tejo Beauty advantages** with specific actions and assets.

Produce:

- **Positioning statement** (1 paragraph + 3 bullets) for Retail and Wholesale.
- **Offer architecture**:
  - Retail: hero offer, bundles, shipping threshold, guarantees.
  - Wholesale: approval flow, MOQ strategy, sample kit, reorder UX, invoicing/terms clarity.
- **Authority system**: proof points (importer credibility, compliance, supplier relationships, reviews, UGC, case studies, trust badges, guarantees).
- **Emotional hook**: brand promise + customer transformation (confidence, ritual, professionalism, savings, reliability).
- **CTAs**: strong, specific CTAs for retail and wholesale.

### C) 90‑Day Plan (execution)

Create a week-by-week plan (Weeks 1–13) to outrun competitors with constrained resources.

Include:

- Weekly outcomes (assets shipped + channels activated)
- KPI targets (leading + lagging)
- Channel strategy for:
  - SEO (topic clusters)
  - Email/SMS (flows + cadence)
  - Paid social (creative angles + offers)
  - Partnerships (micro-influencers, salons/retailers, distributors)
  - Wholesale outbound (lists, scripts, follow-up)

## Constraints / Guardrails

- Must be **ethical and truthful**: use persuasion principles without deception.
- Must work for **EU/Croatia reality**: highlight localization, VAT/invoicing clarity, and cross-border shipping expectations.
- Provide concrete copy and checklists, not generic advice.

## Deliverables (save as Markdown docs)

1. **Competitor Playbook**: competitor table + notes + exploit opportunities.
2. **Messaging Pack**: hero sections (10+ variants), CTAs, value props, proof blocks, objections and rebuttals.
3. **90‑Day Execution Plan**: weekly milestones + KPI dashboard definition.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Names real competitor categories and gives specific, non-handwavy takeaways.
- Produces reusable copy (headlines, subheads, CTAs) for both retail and wholesale.
- 90‑day plan is operational: each week has 3–7 concrete tasks and a measurable outcome.
- Explicitly lists "what most people miss" and turns those into winning moves.
