# Improved Prompt: Tejo Beauty — Wholesale Approval + VAT Clarity Assets

## Objective

Create two short operational assets that increase wholesale conversion by removing uncertainty:

1. a 2-minute wholesale approval checklist (internal + shareable)
2. a plain-language VAT & invoicing explainer (non-legal)

## Context

- Tejo Beauty is a Croatia-based EU importer.
- Wedge: wholesale-first (salons/retailers)
- Rollout: Croatia → Slovenia → Austria → Germany (test)

## Requirements

- The approval checklist must define minimum required information, helpful info, and consistent screening rules.
- The VAT explainer must be short, ethical, and avoid tax advice, while clearly stating what info is needed and what the buyer can expect.
- Both must be copy/paste friendly.

## Deliverables

Save as:

- `tejospec/docs/growth/WHOLESALE_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md`
- `tejospec/docs/growth/VAT_INVOICE_EXPLAINER.md`

Also update the catalog request landing page outline to reference these as optional links.
